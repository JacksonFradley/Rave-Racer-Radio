Rave Racer Remix Idea:
Unyielding aesthetic first
Racing game, built to be felt
Core concepts:
Color
Music
Motion
How do they all affect each other?
Color should be fluid; picking a color for your car is more like picking a vibe, as the courses change, so do the colors
Music affecting color? Race events affecting Music? Speed affecting everything
Motion should be felt. Colors smudge like paint as you hit top speeds, Red line inspiration
Player expression:
Drivers have little character boxes, anime-inspired.
When driver interaction happens, they are supported by the little character boxes having interaction. EX: ramming into another car, the other player's character box rams into yours, some dialogue is exchanged
Rage, Cockiness should have meters that change over the race and as things happen
Every track should feel like a painting: Hiroshi Nagai, etc
Inspirations: Auto modellista, ridge racer type 4
Track DJ: racing should also feel like a boiler room set. Screens of whoever the track DJ is should be posted along the racetrack.
Track DJ should react to moments in the song. This can just be face animations or 
Map of locations + home base. Loading screen is based of a landmark you would logically see on the drive over
