## 2. Reference Library Synthesis (`Reference Images/`)

### 2.1 Shared Silhouette Rule: "Bottom-Heavy, Wide & Angular"
Both the `Cars` (19 images) and `Character Models` (11 images) folders obey the same exaggerated proportion rules:
* **Characters (`Character Models/01`–`10`):** Narrow cropped waists and torsos that **flare aggressively downward** into ultra-baggy cargo/rave pants and **colossal, blocky geometric platform sneakers**, topped by **giant angular polygon hair spikes, forehead goggles, or cybernetic chrome helmets**.
* **Vehicles (`Cars/01`–`19`):** Compact 90s/00s rally hatchbacks, coupes, and drift sedans that are **slammed to the ground with massive, boxy bolt-on widebody fenders, deep-dish or turbofan wheels, meaty poking tires, roof cargo boxes, and oversized multi-plane wings**.

### 2.2 The 5 Aesthetic Sub-Pillars (`Reference Images/Aesthetic/`)
1. **Dreamcast / *Jet Set Radio* Street Pop & Sticker-Bomb (`01_blue_cats`, `02_Wild_Horses`, `04_stickers`, `07_Lets_get_sticky`, `08_Instagram`, `13_DC-UK`):** Thick black ink outlines, feral electric-blue skate cats, die-cut vinyl slap-stickers, 5-point stars, groovy extruded Euro-club/arcade bubble typography.
2. **90s Acid Graphics & Technical Cyber-Brutalism (`06_Untitled`, `14_Amadeus`, `25_CATHAR_EP`, `26_macroblank`, `27_anathena`, `50_cell_phone`, `54_book_cover`):** Fiducial registration targets, measurement rulers, barcodes, QR stamps, military FLIR targeting reticles (`BASS NATION`), bilingual English + Katakana headers, thresholded manga eyes.
3. **Rutt-Etra CRT Scanlines & Hardware Glitch (`05_distortion`, `05_umru`, `18_ASCII`, `57_broken_screen`, `58_polygon1993`, `60_reticle`, `63_womans_face`, `64_beach_glitch`, `65_Archillect`, `66_sb`, `68_sb`):** Glowing cyan/green oscilloscope raster waves, visible RGB sub-pixel matrices, VHS tracking tears, shattered LCD cracks, thermal heat-map eyes.
4. **3D Wireframe Laser Tunnels & Cyberspace Grids (`21_tunnel`, `33_Vaporwave_Dolphin`, `52_chase_heart`, `70_Seres_Cosmicos`, `72_purple_tunnel`, `82_wireframe_hand`, `84_green_tunnel`, `85_NEOMECHANICA`):** Asymmetric 3-sided neon perspective grids (Acid Green `#39FF14` or Electric Violet `#9D00FF`) against pitch-black `#05020A` voids; glowing red/gold wireframe halation.
5. **Thermal Euphoria, Liquid Warp & Chrome Futurism (`03/04/34_Cyber_Masks`, `06_stacked_eyes`, `07/08/09/10/11_gradients`, `20_motion_blur`, `28_red_yellow`, `32_op_art`, `61_Eyes`, `67/69_floating`, `73/79_aura`, `75_chrome_hands`):** Segmented mirror-chrome helmets (Sorayama / Daft Punk), false-color thermal gradients, melting kinetic typography, palette-knife liquid color smearing.
